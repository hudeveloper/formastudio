Clarity & Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our craftsmanship, white-glove logistics, custom upholstery, and guarantees.

What is included in Global White-Glove Delivery?
Every chair is dispatched in custom impact-resistant timber crating. A specialized two-person white-glove team delivers directly to your room of choice, unpacks, conducts multi-point inspection, and positions the chair. All packing debris is removed and 100% recycled at zero additional charge.
How do I clean and maintain the Italian Microfiber Velvet?
Our velvet is woven in Como, Italy, with a microscopic hydrophobic shield. Spills like coffee, wine, or water bead into droplets on the surface and can be blotted immediately with a dry cloth. For routine care, a gentle vacuum brush attachment or soft velvet lint brush preserves its rich dual-tone nap.
What does the 10-Year Warranty cover?
We provide a 10-Year Structural Guarantee covering the solid kiln-dried European beech and ash timber frame, the internal structural steel reinforcement brackets, and the steam-bent arch against warping, sagging, or joint separation. The upholstery fabric carries a 2-year warranty against premature wear.
Can I request physical fabric swatches before ordering?
Absolutely. We offer complimentary swatch boxes containing large samples of all 8 velvet colorways, delivered directly to your doorstep within 48 hours. This allows you to evaluate textures and tones under your home’s natural lighting conditions.
What is your in-home trial and return policy?
We offer a risk-free 30-Day In-Home Trial. Experience the chair in your interior for a full month. If it does not completely harmonize with your space, contact our concierge to schedule a complimentary white-glove pickup for a 100% full refund with zero restocking fees.
What are the exact architectural dimensions & load limits?
Overall Width: 84 cm (33.1") | Total Depth: 82 cm (32.3") | Total Height: 78 cm (30.7") | Seat Height: 44 cm (17.3"). The chair weighs 26 kg (57.3 lbs) and has been certified for dynamic load capacities up to 450 lbs (205 kg).
